protOS facegen

Procedural face generation for a protogen head. Face blendshapes arrive over OSC, a rig maps them onto signed-distance-field shaders, and the GPU renders every LED panel into one atlas. These captures were rendered by the same binary in CI on Mesa's software Vulkan driver, driven by the built-in fake producer, and drawn as the visor is seen from the front. Source: github.com/biscuitvixen/protOS.
